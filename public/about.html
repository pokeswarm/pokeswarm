PokéSwarm

PokéSwarm is a Pokémon survivors-like (Vampire Survivors-like, bullet heaven) roguelite browser game. Survive swarms of wild Pokémon while your moves fire automatically, and grow stronger by picking moves and held items on every level-up.

Play now in your browser (free)

Features

  • Free, no ads, no install. Play in a PC browser or a smartphone browser (landscape).
  • Online co-op for up to 4 players. Share a room URL or invite friends to play together.
  • Survivors-like action. You only move; your moves fire on their own. Enemies grow in number over time until swarms fill the screen.
  • Roguelite builds. Every run brings a different mix of moves and held items. Max out a move and pair it with the right item to evolve it into a stronger move. Coins earned in runs buy permanent upgrades.
  • Pokémon at heart. Type matchups, STAB, critical hits, evolution by level and by evolution stones, gym-leader and Elite Four style bosses, and battles against legendary Pokémon.
  • 54 playable Pokémon across the Kanto and Johto regions (Hoenn in progress).
  • 6 languages: English, 日本語, Français, Deutsch, Español and Italiano.

How to play

  1. Open pokeswarm.org and choose SOLO or MULTI.
  2. Pick a region and a Pokémon to start a run.
  3. Move with WASD or the arrow keys (on smartphones, the joystick on the left side of the screen). Attacks are automatic.
  4. Collect experience orbs to level up, then choose a move or held item from the offers.
  5. Defeat each section's boss to move on. Beat the final boss to clear the run.

FAQ

What kind of game is it?

It combines survivors-like (Vampire Survivors-like, bullet heaven) action with roguelite builds that change every run. The theme is Pokémon.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Turn your phone sideways and play in the browser. Add it to your home screen to play in full screen.

Is there co-op (multiplayer)?

Yes. Up to 4 players can play together, connected directly browser to browser.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and no purchases. There is an optional Ko-fi for support, but it never gives any in-game advantage.

Is PokéSwarm the same as PokéSurvivor?

No. PokéSurvivor is a separate fan game by a different creator and is not related to PokéSwarm. Both are Pokémon-themed survivors-like browser games.

Is it an official game?

No. It is an unofficial, non-profit fan game and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.
